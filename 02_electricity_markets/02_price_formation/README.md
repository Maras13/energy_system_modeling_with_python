# 02 Price formation

| Notebook | Lesson | Topic |
|---|---|---|
| `02_price_formation.ipynb` | M2.2 | A synthetic, Croatia-sized year (18.4 TWh) cleared hour by hour with a vectorised merit order and checked against a full-year PyPSA model in all 8,760 hours; the merit-order effect; capture prices, capture rates and cannibalisation (why solar loses value faster than wind); negative prices and how support rules change them; marginal costs with a CO₂ price and the coal-to-gas switching price, predicted by hand and confirmed by simulation |

Exercises are in `exercise/`, the answer key in `exercise/solutions/` (submit your own answers first).
The exercise finds which technology sets the price how often, the capacity at which cannibalisation
bites, and shows how a flexible electrolyser absorbs negative prices (full-year PyPSA model).

All data are **synthetic** and all fuel and technology numbers **illustrative**.
