# Further learning: videos and notebooks that fit this course

## Data Science for Energy System Modelling (Fabian Neumann, TU Berlin)

Fabian Neumann is one of the core developers of PyPSA and teaches this course at TU Berlin. His
lectures and workshops are recorded on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@fabianneumann5386)
([full playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PL9_USGH2eQENhhCH9md2bJDNKPTIISWgd)); slides
and notebooks are on the [course website](https://fneum.github.io/data-science-for-esm/). The website
content is licensed CC-BY 4.0 — credit Fabian Neumann if you reuse it.

Use it as **optional parallel viewing**: watch the matching video before or after a lesson here for a
second explanation from a different angle. This course stays the main thread — it adds the
foundations, the Croatian / South-East European case study, exercises with answer keys and the
portfolio projects.
